(2) Problems of availability

    (2.1) ActTrq - Misalignment L2 and L1 in regard to use of AirMod_ratChrgAirCylAdv_a instead of rlp_w
       * specification of the problem(s)
		Use of AirMod_ratChrgAirCylAdv_a[Gen_ctCylAdv] for access to KFZWOP (model reference ignition angle at lambda=1) and KFZMIOP differs from L2. 
		This deviation is expected to be very small and only during situations with high dynamic can be eventually noticiable. 
		This differences might lead to different calculation of actual torque between L2 and L1.               
       * remedy
	     Update L2 or calibrate L1 such that the functional differences are not active.
	     Check while calibration that the differences are not very high (<15%) and of short duration (<100ms). 	
      * evaluation
            Missdetection is possible if changes/recomendation are not followed.
      * clearing
        * date: 09.10.2024
        * participants: Tamas Anton-Horatiu (PS-EC/ESF3), Deepthi Reddy (MS/EPJ34-PS), Fleckenstein Birgit (PS-EC/ESC2)
        * decision: Not applicable in current project.

   (2.2) Gear-dependent Drive Mode and Drive Mode ramping different in Lv 1 and Lv2
    * specification of the problem(s)
	In Lv 2 there are two modes (Eco and Sport) which are independent from gearmode. 
	In Lv 1 AccPed_TqCalcn there are individual gear dependent maps for several drive modes:
	Gear dependent drive mode  
	In Lv 1 ramping between the modes is gear dependent, in Lv 2 not.
    * remedy
	It needs to be ensured, that Lv 2 Drive Mode covers all the gear dependent maps of Lv 1. But the gap between Lv 1 and Lv 2 shall not lead to 
	an non-acceptable increase of permitted torque. Therefore the calibration values of the Lv 1 maps shall not deviate to much between the different gears. 
	The calibration of the mode ramping in Lv 2 shall cover the gear dependency of Lv 1 
    * clearing
        * date: 15.10.2024
        * participants: Deepthi Reddy (MS/EPJ34-PS) , Fleckenstein Birgit (PS-EC/ESC2)
        * decision: No usage of Drive Modes in 62XS, is clarified with CE-team
    * status 
            clearing is {done}
